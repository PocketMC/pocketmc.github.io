Connectivity

Discord Integration

Connect your desktop client with Discord for live activity status, player join notifications, and direct DM delivery of remote control access tokens.

Discord Rich Presence (RPC)

Show your friends what you are hosting in real-time. PocketMC provides deep Rich Presence customization with an in-app live preview card:

  • Display active Minecraft version, server engine type, and uptime.
  • Show current connected player counts (e.g. Playing Survival (4/20)).
  • Add interactive buttons with direct links to your server's Discord or website.

PocketMC Welcome Bot

PocketMC operates an official community Discord bot (pocketmc-welcome-bot). When paired with your desktop client:

  1. The bot verifies server ownership and assigns member roles in your Discord server.
  2. When your PC reboots or launches, the bot DMs you your active Playit tunnel and remote web access URL.
  3. Crash alerts and memory warnings can be broadcasted to designated staff channels.
